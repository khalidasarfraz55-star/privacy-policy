PalettePop

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Summary

PalettePop does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app works entirely on your device, has no account system and does not connect to the internet.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the PalettePop Android app (package name com.khalidasarfraz.palettepop0), published by Khalid Sarfraz (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. In particular, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details
  • Your location
  • Photos, contacts, files or microphone and camera data
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage analytics
  • Payment information

3. Data stored on your device

To make the app work, the following information is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • The palette you are currently working on and its mode
  • Palettes you choose to save, with their names and collections (Branding, App UI, Nature, Personal)
  • Your list of recently used palettes

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can remove it at any time by deleting palettes inside the app, by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Clipboard

When you tap a color value (HEX, RGB or HSL), the app copies that value to your device clipboard. The app only writes to the clipboard after your tap and never reads clipboard content.

5. Sample screens

The Studio section shows sample screens (a website, a profile card and a form) only to preview your colors. They are not functional: the app has no sign-in, no accounts, and does not accept, send or store any input from these sample screens.

6. Permissions

PalettePop does not request any special Android permissions, and it does not request internet access.

7. Sharing and third parties

We do not share data with any third party because we do not collect any. The app does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not include third-party services that process personal data.

8. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Data security

Because your palettes remain on your device, their security depends on your device protections such as screen lock and system updates. We recommend keeping your device up to date.

10. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You are in full control of the data stored inside the app on your device.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
